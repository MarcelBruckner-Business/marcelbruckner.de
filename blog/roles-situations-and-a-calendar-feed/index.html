Roles, situations and a calendar feed

Over the last two days freundebuch learned the difference between who someone is to us and what’s going on in their life. It also got a calendar feed, and Claude now gets told what to do after each call.

Roles are relationships now

“Our landlady” used to be filed as a fact, right next to the news about her new sink. Now it’s a relationship to the household: to “me” or to “us”. It’s written in plain German words for both ends (“Vermieterin” / “Mieter:in”) instead of a fixed type. People can have an optional gender, so the page reads “Unsere Vermieterin”. Claude only sets a gender when I say it or use a pronoun, never from the name. When a gender changes, Claude is told which words need regendering. That’s now only the neutral ones: setting 11 genders on my data listed 2 words to fix instead of 18.

Situations that nudge

Facts are split into two kinds. Gut zu wissen holds lasting ones (job, kids, allergies). Gerade los holds situations (a trip, a renovation). Each situation has a day to check in again. On that day it moves to the top of the page and into the daily push: “💬 Frag mal nach: Anna – sucht ein Auto”. Without a date it’s six weeks. When there’s news, Claude suggests a new day and agrees it with me before filing.

The person page also draws a small network: the household on top, the person in the middle, their people below, with a label on every line.

Tool results say what’s next

Every MCP tool result now carries next: short instructions built from what the call actually did. One example: “this situation nudges on 8 December, a default; tell the user.” Another: “two people match, ask which one.” Before, that guidance lived in the tool descriptions, where Claude had to remember it before it knew what the call had done. Now it arrives right after the call.

Also new

  • Calendar feed: birthdays (with the age when the year is known), other dates, and your own promises that have a due date, as an ICS URL you can subscribe to. Google refreshes it only every few hours, sometimes once a day.
  • Either of us can fix a shared item, not just whoever recorded it. Making an item private stays with the person who recorded it.
  • Softer background: slow colour bubbles that stand still if you’ve turned motion off.

Changelog on GitHub. Tell me what you’d want from the nudges.
